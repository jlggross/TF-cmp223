- Intel SpeedStep Technology: Disabled - This deature allows the sustem to dynammicaly adjust processor voltage and core frequency, decreasing average power consumption and heat production.

- Intel Turbo Boost Technology: Disabled - This option enables or disables the Intel TurboBoost mode of the processor.
* Disabled: Does not allow the TurboBoost driver to increase the performance state of the processor above the standard performance.
* Enabled: Allows the Intel TurboBoost driver to increase the performance of the CPU or graphics processor.
